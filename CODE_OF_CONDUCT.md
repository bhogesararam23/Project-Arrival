# Code of Conduct

Project Arrival aims to be a respectful, constructive and evidence-driven project.

Participants must:
- Be respectful and professional.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and trade-offs.
- Protect private information and credentials.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and personal attacks.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when relevant.

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from project participation.
