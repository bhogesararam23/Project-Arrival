# Provider and Data-Source Research

**Snapshot date:** 2026-11-23. Official documentation reviewed; this is a technical screen, not legal approval, contract quote, coverage guarantee or vendor recommendation. Recheck terms, prices, features and coverage before procurement.

## Decision summary

No one source substantiates a global, reusable, accurate restaurant index plus global ordering. Keep a source portfolio and field-level provenance. Google Places is a compliant on-demand lookup option, not a bulk Arrival index. OpenStreetMap (OSM) is an open dataset with attribution/share-alike obligations and uneven volunteer coverage; public Nominatim and tile servers are not high-volume production infrastructure. Foursquare Places is a potential contracted source whose exact rights, field coverage and rates require confirmation. Merchant submissions and authorized POS systems are the authority for orderable menus and acceptance.

No payment, POS, maps or delivery provider is selected. Choose a pilot based on merchant location and operating model, then verify access, market eligibility, legal terms, cost and support.

## Geography and source policy

Store ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 market identifiers and ISO 4217 currency codes, but maintain dated evidence-backed capability records at the granularity a provider grants: country, city, merchant, service-area polygon or quote-time result. Country support in one API proves nothing about another API, merchant activation, payout ability or a specific address. Serviceability quote results are expiring evidence; do not turn provider driving ETAs into an independent model without permission.

For admin boundaries, evaluate licensed government/open datasets or self-hosted OSM-derived data with separate provenance. Do not derive restaurant inclusion from Google coordinates. Geocoding must explicitly record if returned coordinates may persist. Prefer merchant coordinates or sources whose terms allow durable storage. Do not send personal addresses to providers without contract and privacy review.

## Cost and commercial diligence

Prices above are limited to values expressly shown in cited docs at snapshot time. Exclusions include tax, FX, contractual minimums, enterprise tiers, platform/transaction/dispute fees, delivery price, onboarding and operating/legal cost. Obtain written quotes and measure request mix. Add budget caps, alerts, request minimization, rate limits, only-permitted caching and cost per completed order.

## Unresolved questions

1. Which country/city and merchant are the pilot? This controls every shortlist.
2. Which entity contracts with diner and restaurant; who is seller of record and settles funds?
3. Can the chosen merchant integration accept external orders, return authoritative acceptance and support cancel/refund/partial fulfillment?
4. Which license permits a persistent cross-provider index and derived ranking? Is ODbL compatible with intended database and service?
5. Which provider fields may be retained, for how long, and what happens on source withdrawal/termination?
6. Which tax, fee display, allergen, consumer, privacy, localization and residency rules apply?
7. Which delivery coverage, quote guarantees, location permissions and insurance apply?

Downloadability or API access alone is not legal permission for a production use.
