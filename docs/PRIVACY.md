# Privacy Principles

## Product commitments

- Anonymous browsing by default.
- No mandatory GPS permission.
- Manual destination selection.
- No background customer-location tracking.
- Optional accounts.
- Data minimization and purpose limitation.
- Clear disclosures for information shared with merchants, payment providers and delivery operators.
- Defined retention periods and deletion workflows.
- Appropriate handling of financial, tax and dispute records that must be retained.

## Important limitation

A real delivery cannot be anonymous to every participant. Merchants and fulfilment providers may need a delivery address, recipient name, contact channel or access instructions. Payment providers may process transaction and fraud-prevention information.

Arrival should minimize its own collection and restrict access, not promise impossible end-to-end anonymity.

## Data inventory to maintain

For each field, document: purpose, legal basis where applicable, source, controller/processor role, recipients, retention, access controls, deletion behavior, and cross-border transfer considerations.

Market-specific privacy review is a launch gate.
